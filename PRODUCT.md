# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Star Wars: Unlimited players who want limited-format reps — draft and sealed —
without needing a physical playgroup, product on hand, or an event to attend.
They range from newcomers learning how limited works to experienced grinders
preparing between events. Typical context: at home or on a phone, often in a
spare pocket of time, wanting to get into a pod and start picking cards quickly.
Many arrive solo and fill empty seats with bots; others run multiplayer pods
with friends over a shared link.

## Product Purpose

Protect the Pod ("SWUPOD") is a free, fan-made Star Wars: Unlimited draft and
sealed simulator with an integrated deck builder. It generates booster packs to
the official pack distribution and odds, runs real-time multiplayer drafts
(2–8 players, bot-fillable), supports sealed pool generation, and lets players
build, sort, and export decks from what they open. Success looks like a player
going from landing to opening their first pack in seconds, trusting that the
packs mirror the real game, and coming back for repeated practice.

## Brand Personality

Confident, cinematic, and unpretentious. The voice is a knowledgeable friend at
the table, not a corporation and not a hype machine. Three words: **cinematic,
frictionless, trustworthy.** The interface should evoke the anticipation of
cracking real packs (dark, glowing, high-production) while staying calm and
out of the way everywhere that isn't a moment worth celebrating. It welcomes
newcomers without talking down to grinders.

## Anti-references

- **Not a generic SaaS dashboard.** No light, flat, gray-on-white admin-panel
  look. This is a game tool, not a B2B analytics app.
- **Not cluttered or spreadsheet-y.** Avoid dense data-table overload and walls
  of competing controls. The cards and the act of playing come first; data
  supports, never buries.
- **Not official / affiliated-looking.** Must read as fan-made and distinct from
  FFG / Disney official Star Wars: Unlimited branding. The product is
  unaffiliated and should never imply otherwise.
- **Not childish or cartoonish.** No toy-like, over-animated, playful styling.
  It's for players who take limited seriously.

## Design Principles

1. **Reps over ceremony.** Getting into a draft or sealed pool is the core loop;
   minimize clicks, defaults, and setup friction between landing and first pick.
   Respect the player's time — a practice tool earns return visits by being fast.
2. **Cinematic where it counts, calm everywhere else.** The pack-opening moment
   earns spectacle (glow, motion, reveal); routine surfaces (deck builder,
   history, settings) stay quiet and legible. Spectacle is a budget, not a
   default — spend it on the moments that matter.
3. **Trust through visible accuracy.** The simulation mirrors the real game's
   odds and card data; surface that fidelity (real distributions, honest counts,
   correct rarities) so players believe what they're practicing against.
4. **A welcoming on-ramp, deep enough to stay.** Newcomers should understand what
   to do without a manual; grinders shouldn't feel the experience was dumbed down
   for them. Progressive disclosure over either extreme.
5. **Distinctly ours, unmistakably Star Wars-adjacent.** Lean into the dark,
   space-y, glowing identity without imitating official branding. The fan-made
   character is a feature, not an apology.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Solid, pragmatic baseline (not formally certified):

- **Contrast:** target WCAG AA — body text ≥ 4.5:1, large/bold text ≥ 3:1 —
  against the dark surfaces. Watch glow-on-dark and gray-on-black combinations.
- **Keyboard:** full keyboard navigation for core flows (drafting, deck
  building, dialogs); visible focus states.
- **Motion:** honor `prefers-reduced-motion` — pack-opening and glow animations
  need a reduced-motion alternative (crossfade or instant).
- **Touch / mobile:** comfortable touch targets and responsive layouts; the app
  is used heavily on phones.
- **Color independence:** don't rely on aspect color alone to convey meaning
  (pair with icon, label, or shape) for color-blind players.
