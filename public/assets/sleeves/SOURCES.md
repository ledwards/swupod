# Sleeve artwork catalog

Research date: 2026-10-04. Scope: official Gamegenic Star Wars: Unlimited sleeve designs. Selection affects local presentation only, not ownership or match state.

The per-design source page, original image URL, dimensions, and display crop are recorded in `src/data/sleeves.json`. Original downloaded files are retained unchanged. SVG view boxes isolate the sleeve from official product-photo backgrounds without resampling or inventing detail.

## Included

- 44 retail designs from [Gamegenic’s SWU catalog](https://www.gamegenic.com/product-category/license/starwarsunlimited/?product_count=100): core card backs and patterns, Spark of Rebellion, Shadows of the Galaxy, Twilight of the Republic, Jump to Lightspeed, Legends of the Force, Secrets of Power, A Lawless Time, A Spark of Hope, and Homeworlds. These are manufacturer-listed designs; stock/release availability is not asserted.
- Six full-resolution prize-wall sleeve images and Mace Windu Day 2 participation sleeves from the [2025 Galactic Championship prize announcement](https://starwarsunlimited.com/articles/galactic-prizes).
- Chimaera and Bo-Katan prize-wall sleeves and Obi-Wan LCQ participation sleeves from the [2026 Galactic Championship prizes](https://galacticchampionship.starwarsunlimited.com/2026/prizes).

Retail source images are 1200px product renders, with approximately 500×700px sleeve artwork. Six 2025 prize-wall originals are 718×1000 or larger. The four remaining event designs use the best official composite images located (222–275px-wide sleeve areas); these are adequate at table size, but should be replaced if larger originals become available. No artificial detail has been generated or claimed as original artwork.

## Known gaps

The [Season 0 Regional announcement](https://starwarsunlimited.com/articles/sectors-and-regionals-season-0) confirms Executor, Plo Koon, and Ahsoka Tano sleeves. Its composite shows partially occluded, tilted, low-resolution art. These are not selectable until a complete usable image is available.

No champion-exclusive sleeve design was verified. The Champion section remains empty instead of misclassifying general Galactic Championship merchandise as winner-only prizes.

## Ordering

Card backs: original → retail (core then release order) → prize wall → participation → champion. This is a presentation tier, not a market-price ranking. Token cards: Standard → Standard Foil → Hyperspace → Hyperspace Foil → GC Participation → GC VIP Promo. Equal-tier printings retain source order.
